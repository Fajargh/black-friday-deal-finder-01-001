Every deal below was found and checked by hand on Black Friday itself: the product, the store, the sale price, the usual price, and an honest verdict on whether it is really a bargain.

How this board checks a deal

  • Read, not guessed. Each price was read on 27 Nov 2026 at the store or in the deals source named on the deal card. The “usual price” is the one that source itself stated.
  • An honest verdict. NEW LOW means the lowest price ever tracked. STRONG DEAL is a real cut, well under list. GOOD PRICE saves money but a rival store beats it. ROUTINE SALE is a small cut — think twice. REFURB STEAL is cheapest, but refurbished, not new.
  • Same product, two stores. Where the board tracked one product at two stores, the deal page compares them, so you buy the cheaper one.
  • Prices move. Sale prices can change or sell out within hours. Check the store link before you buy — the link goes to the store's own search for that product.
